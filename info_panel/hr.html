Hostage Rescue


Overview

For an assessment as part of my A-Level studies, I created a first-person shooter in the Unity game engine. The main gameplay loop is based around locating and destroying holographic robots in a procedurally-generated environment. The goal of the game is to clear all rooms of enemies and reach and end goal (in this game, a holographic 'hostage' robot) in as little time as possible, gaining more points for better accuracy.

It had a development time of around seven months, developed solo and balanced alongside my two additional A-Level studies. Development time included creating full documentation of the project, market research, forward planning, and an evaluation of the project upon its completion. Additionally, liasing with stakeholders and applying feedback were critical elements of the development process.

Features

  • Procedurally-generated levels
  • Adaptive enemy artificial intelligence
